# Nora 🎮

Nora lets you launch games and videos using physical NFC media.

Got a card, disc or cartridge? Just tap it on the reader and Nora will open the content linked to it.

### What do you need?

- An ESP32 board.
- An NFC reader module (such as RC522).
- NFC cards or tags.
- A device running Nora.

### What can you do with it?

- Make collectible game cards.
- Create physical copies of your favorite games.
- Launch movies and videos from custom discs.
- Build your own NFC cartridges and other media.

Give your digital collection a physical form!
